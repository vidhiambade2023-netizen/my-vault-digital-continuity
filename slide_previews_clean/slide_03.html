The Digital Continuity Hub          03 Problem Space
03
Our digital lives are scattered across dozens of services.
When crisis strikes, access is fragmented and opaque.
ACCUMULATION PATTERNS
The Everyday Footprint
People accumulate thousands of assets across isolated services with zero unified overview.
  • Memories: Photos and videos locked in iCloud and Google Photos
  • Legal Records: Tax documents and deeds scattered in cloud drives
  • Communications: Decades of conversations in WhatsApp and Gmail
  • Financial Portals: Banking portals, utility accounts, and subscriptions
ECOSYSTEM FRICTION
The Silent Impasse
Current platform policies force families into painful dilemmas during incapacity or loss.
  • Platform Silos: Apple, Google, and Meta have disconnected legacy rules
  • Binary Access Trap: Platforms either lock everyone out or hand over master keys
  • Emotional Avoidance: Macabre death-oriented tools induce user paralysis
  • Procedural Deadlock: Families face months of expensive court bureaucracy
