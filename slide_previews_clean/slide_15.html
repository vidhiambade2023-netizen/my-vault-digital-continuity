Evidence-Driven Design Refinements
REFINEMENT 01
1. Link vs Share
Initial Friction: Users feared linking Google Drive immediately shared files with family.
  • Issue: Conflation of authentication and delegation
  • Response: Re-architected into 2 distinct steps
  • Added badge: 'Connecting does not share files'
REFINEMENT 02
2. Zero-Trust Defaults
Initial Friction: Open checklists created anxiety over accidental over-sharing.
  • Issue: Manual exclusion induces fear and fatigue
  • Response: Chats and passwords locked by default
  • User must explicitly unlock private categories
REFINEMENT 03
3. Plain Triggers
Initial Friction: Users were bewildered by technical OAuth timeout jargon.
  • Issue: Technical terms alienated non-technical users
  • Response: Replaced with conversational conditions
  • 'Access released only after 90 days inactivity'
