Four Guiding Pillars for Intentional Continuity
PILLAR 01
Selective Delegation
Access is never binary. Users configure permissions per category (e.g. photos vs legal certificates).
PILLAR 02
Privacy-by-Default
Private chats, SMS backups, and passwords are permanently locked and excluded by default.
PILLAR 03
Plain Scoping
Transparent explanations of what connecting a service or designating a contact enables.
PILLAR 04
Instant Revocation
Users maintain absolute sovereignty to edit, freeze, or cancel permissions at any time.
