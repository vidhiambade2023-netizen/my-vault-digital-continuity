Investigating Digital Mental Models Across Generations
METHODOLOGY & SAMPLING
Primary Research Instruments
A mixed-methods inquiry exploring storage habits, retrieval friction, and custodian trust.
  • Quantitative Instrument: 15-question Google Form surveying account volume, storage habits, and privacy thresholds
  • Qualitative Protocol: 6 cross-generational interview profiles: Student (15y), College (22y), Professional (36y), Doctor (47y), Academic (52y), Senior (75y)
  • Core Inquiry: What would you want trusted people to access, and what must remain permanently private?
ACADEMIC HONESTY
Research Transparency Disclosure
The quantitative figures presented in this case study reflect the structured exploratory pilot dataset (n=100) established for pattern analysis.
