Initial Assumption vs. Research-Led Reality
HYPOTHESIS A
Initial Academic Assumption
People need a 'digital will' to transfer all online accounts after death.
  • Assumes digital legacy is purely a post-mortem legal event
  • Assumes users want to hand over full master credentials to family
  • Treats personal data as financial property to be inherited as a monolith
EVIDENCE-LED REALITY
Observed Research Reality
People reject blanket transfer. They demand selective delegation with strict boundaries.
  • The gap appears before death: users cannot organize what they cannot see
  • Users want family to access photos, but dread exposing private chats
  • Access is not binary: users demand granular, category-level scoping
  • Privacy and revocability are absolute preconditions for user trust
