Visual Identity Tokens & Swiss Typography
SURFACE TOKENS
Color Palette
  • Obsidian #0D0D0D: Contrast & focus
  • Canvas #FFFFFF: Airy readability
  • Slate Mist #EEF0F2: Subtle structure
  • Emerald #10B981: Active status
  • Crimson #EF4444: Boundary lock
NEO-GROTESQUE SCALE
Typography
  • Display (44pt Bold): Impact titles
  • Section Head (24pt): Signposts
  • Card Header (14pt Bold): Anchors
  • Body (10pt Regular): 1.4 line-height
  • Running Meta (8.5pt): Uppercase
LAYOUT LOGIC
Grid & Hairlines
  • 8pt Baseline Grid alignment
  • Hairline 0.5pt divider rules
  • Rectangular containers with sharp edges
  • Generous negative space throughout
ETHICAL STANDARDS
Calm Tone
  • WCAG AAA Contrast compliance
  • Zero macabre death metaphors
  • Reassuring, dignified microcopy
  • No dark patterns or forced signups
